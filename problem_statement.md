## AI agent for warehouse data management and real time report to supervisors' queries.
1. The company needs fast and accurate answers to stock questions from supervisors, especially for specific products across multiple warehouses.
2. Today, stock reporting depends too much on warehouse staff manually checking records, systems, and sometimes physical stock.
3. The biggest problem is the gap between actual stock movement and system stock records.
4. Daily outbound stock is hard to track accurately because items may be sold, reserved, borrowed, or returned without timely updates.
5. Delayed sales documents and informal handling create frequent discrepancies between what is physically in the warehouse and what the system says.
6. Inbound stock is usually more controlled, but the process is slow, labor-intensive, and small mistakes can take a long time to fix.
7. Internal transfers between warehouses may be recorded, but tracing the full history of a product still wastes a lot of time.
8. The current process relies heavily on warehouse keepers having exceptional memory, discipline, and attention to detail.
9. This creates business risk, because warehouse roles can be unstable, and performance can vary from person to person.
10. Even when data is available, extracting the right information quickly for supervisors is still difficult and time-consuming.
11. An AI agent can help by centralizing stock movement data, checking stock status instantly, and giving quick answers in a usable format.
12. To make this work, we must improve the recording process together first, then build the AI agent on top of cleaner and more reliable stock data.
